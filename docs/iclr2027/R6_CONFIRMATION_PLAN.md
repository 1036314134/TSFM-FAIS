# Independent confirmation of the fixed shared forecast gate

The R5 gate and its settings are fixed before new cohort construction. The method binding is `artifacts/iclr27-r6/method-freeze-v001/manifest.json`, SHA256 `a504316976e9f0782e8f1a9542066c5ac12fc5b27387c042e39d10613e0cbb40`. It references all 12 unchanged source-trained checkpoints. The primary method averages the three ensemble-loss gate outputs; the three member-loss gates are the matched control. The forecasters, imputers, source-only fitting, 33 features and original inference recipe remain fixed. No new method tuning is permitted on this confirmation.

The primary horizon is 96 steps with a 96-step context. A prespecified secondary horizon of 192 steps tests horizon transfer without retraining the source gate. Both horizons use the same eligible histories and the same realized context masks. Keep the primary training-prefix-standardized MAE/MSE and original future observation masks. No overall favorable horizon may be selected after evaluation. Runtime adapters and the scoring implementation for the longer horizon must be checked before forecasting the cohort.

Four candidate public sources are selected before forecasting accuracy is examined. Data eligibility and parsing are reviewed first; actual cohort size is then frozen. A source that cannot satisfy the declared time/observation conditions is reported as excluded rather than replaced according to performance.

| Source | Numerical targets, first in evaluation order | Remaining measurements and exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| UCI Beijing Multi-Site Air Quality, 501 | PM2.5, PM10 | SO2, NO2, CO, O3, TEMP, PRES, DEWP, RAIN, WSPM; exclude row IDs, calendar fields, station identifiers and categorical wind direction |
| UCI Appliances Energy Prediction, 374 | Appliances, lights | Keep the measured environmental channels; exclude the provider's two deliberately random variables rv1 and rv2 |
| UCI Bike Sharing, 275, hourly release | casual, registered | Retain temp, atemp, hum and windspeed; exclude count total cnt, which is the sum of the targets, and identifiers/calendar/categorical fields |
| UCI Occupancy Detection, 357 | Temperature, Humidity | Retain Light and CO2; exclude the occupancy classification label, row IDs and HumidityRatio, which is derived from the two target variables |

These target and input selections are based on source semantics, not forecast errors. They define a multivariate forecasting task for the recorded measurements; the occupancy classification label is not a forecasting input or target. Provider-supplied scaling and interpolation, where applicable, are recorded. New to selector development does not imply absent from forecasting-model pretraining.

Use all 12 Beijing stations as separate items within one source family for original-missingness evaluation. For the synthetic panel, use the first four station names in lexical order, with no station selection by predictive performance. The other releases each form one item. Merge the three occupancy files chronologically, ignoring their original classification train/test roles. Use the nominal time grids: Beijing and Bike Sharing hourly, Appliances every 10 minutes, Occupancy every minute. Occupancy timestamps may be rounded to their nearest minute only when every rounding displacement is at most one second; multiple measurements at a rounded minute are averaged, with counts and displacements preserved. Larger discrepancies require source review. Other sources require exact alignment to the declared grid. No calendar origin or timezone is invented.

Originally missing Beijing measurements retain NA. Missing time bins in other sources remain unobserved rather than being filled with zero. Their cause is not assumed to be sensor failure: absent bike records may reflect reporting conventions, and clock effects may affect nominal time bins. Consequently, source-NA histories and unrecorded-time-bin histories are separate secondary panels. Synthetic evaluation uses genuinely observed contexts and observed target futures. It never treats an inserted value as ground truth.

Source-fit and scoring prefixes use the first 20% of each item with at least two original observations in every retained channel. Candidate origins start after the 60% boundary plus the context, and after the prefix plus one context and two maximum horizons. Use stride 288 (context 96 plus maximum horizon 192). Require at least 48 observed values per target in the first 96 future steps and at least 96 in the first 192. For natural or unrecorded-bin panels, retain up to 16 missing-context and 16 complete-context origins per item, selected evenly in chronological order within strata. These are conditional panels, not estimates of population missingness frequency.

For each synthetic item, choose up to eight evenly spaced eligible origins with a complete 96-step multivariate history and complete two-target futures through step 192. Use all six existing mechanisms, rates 0.1/0.3/0.5 and new seeds 8101/8102. Generate masks over the full trajectory with prefix-only calibration, retain original observation masks, and then extract the registered contexts. Keep zero-missing realized contexts if the global masking realization produces them; report realized rates. Do not count 36 masks of one history as 36 independent histories.

Before forecasting, verify source IDs against prior source and confirmation catalogs, preserve archive and parsed-array hashes, and compare exact target-history/future content with prior evaluated windows. Freeze the complete task IDs, source paths, prefix statistics and missing masks. Retain all strong controls: seven individual forecasts, source-fixed and original learned portfolios, means/medians, the small gate's source-fixed convex and single controls, standalone MoTM and its eight-member median. The latter may require one additional candidate query; budget differences are reported explicitly.

Report each horizon, source family and missingness panel separately, with complete data/count tables and failures. The primary claim concerns the fixed method on the new cohort at horizon 96; horizon 192 and original-missingness/gap analyses test its limits. Inspecting the results makes this cohort used. Any subsequent change requires its evaluation role to be relabeled and cannot be called another independent confirmation on the same data.
