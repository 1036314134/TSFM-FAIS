# Post-evaluation teacher-transfer diagnostic

The audited follow-up has mixed results. The primary selector improves both errors against the seven-candidate median on the two Chronos synthetic sources, while TimesFM loses on both. Chronos also trades lower MAE for higher MSE against the source-fixed triple. This diagnostic is specified after seeing those results and is explicitly explanatory; it is not another independent confirmation or a deployable method.

Use exactly the 576 registered synthetic cases from the same 16 weather/Solar histories. Query each frozen forecaster once on each complete original history, before the artificial masking, giving at most 32 additional history forecasts across the two models. The original raw input scale, 96-step context/horizon, two targets and prefix scoring standardizers remain fixed. Natural-missing sources are excluded because their unobserved clean histories are not available; no fill is substituted for clean truth.

Retain the learned portfolio, matched member-risk control, original individual-teacher rule, source-fixed triple and seven-candidate median. Add unavailable reference outputs: the clean-history forecast itself, the triple closest to that forecast, individual teacher-ranked top three, and separate future MAE/MSE oracles over the seven singles and 35 triples. Chronos preserves a shared choice; TimesFM permits separate target choices. Use the existing tested oracle helpers and the same sorted seven-action catalog.

The direct teacher-optimal triple must have teacher MSE no larger than the learned triple or ideal individual-top-three triple. All true-future metrics are auxiliary diagnostics of these unavailable references. A gap to the teacher optimum is not automatically reducible learning error; disagreement between teacher and true-future optima also reflects forecaster error and the restricted candidate class. The two source families and 16 histories do not identify a universal cause of transfer failure. No method parameter, candidate set or primary result is changed in this diagnostic.
